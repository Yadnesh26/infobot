InfoBot

Got a suspicious forward? Send it to InfoBot on WhatsApp and get a short, sourced answer back.

How it works

  • Forward the message to InfoBot. Text, screenshots, voice notes and short videos all work.
  • InfoBot reads it (it can read text inside images and transcribe audio), works out the claim being made, and checks it.
  • You get a reply quoted against your original message, with a verdict, how confident the check is, and links to the sources it used.
  • The reply is written to be safe to forward back into the group where the rumour started.

Languages

English, Hindi and Marathi, including Hindi or Marathi typed in English letters.

Important limits.
  • InfoBot is an information tool, not a doctor. It does not give medical advice.
  • For questions about doses, medicines, symptoms or a specific health condition, it will not try to answer and will point you to a doctor or pharmacist instead.
  • It can be wrong. Where sources are missing or disagree, it says so instead of guessing. Please use your own judgement.

Status

InfoBot is currently in testing and not yet open to the public.
