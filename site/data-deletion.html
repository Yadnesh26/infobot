Delete my data

You can ask us to delete everything InfoBot holds that is linked to your phone number.

How to ask

  1. Email decoderop@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data.
  2. Include the phone number you messaged InfoBot from, with country code (for example +91 98XXXXXXXX). We need it because we only store a one-way hash of it, and can only find your records by hashing the number you give us.
  3. Send the email from an address you can be reached at, so we can confirm when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Every request record tied to your number: message IDs, message types, timestamps and statuses.
  • Any emoji reactions you gave to our replies.
  • Your per-hour usage counters.

What stays

The short English summaries of claims we have already checked, with their verdicts and sources, are stored without any link to you. They stay, because they are the shared answers other people receive for the same rumour. If you believe one contains your personal information, tell us in the same email and we will remove it.

How long it takes

We aim to delete your records within 30 days and will email you when it is done.
